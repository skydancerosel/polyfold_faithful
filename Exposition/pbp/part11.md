## Part XI. Geometry up to equivalences (HWZ Chapter 11, §§11.1–11.6 (2017 §§11.1–11.6))

Chapter 11 closes the ep-groupoid half of Part II; its numbers and pages are those of the book (Springer 2021, pp. 521–566), with the 2017 text's in parentheses (2017 pp. 375–416). It asks which of the notions built on ep-groupoids in Chapters 8 and 9 survive the passage to $\mathcal{EP}(E^{-1})$ of Chapter 10, where generalized isomorphisms are the isomorphisms. §11.1 extends the tangent functor (Theorem 11.1.1 (2017: 11.1)), shows that the degeneracy index is invariant (Proposition 11.1.2 (2017: 11.1)) and treats faces (Definition 11.1.3, Example 11.1.4, Remark 11.1.5, Proposition 11.1.6, Remark 11.1.7, Proposition 11.1.8 (2017: Definition 11.1, Example 11.1, Remark 11.1, Proposition 11.2, Remark 11.2, Proposition 11.3)). §11.2 does the same for sc-differential forms (Theorem 11.2.1 to Proposition 11.2.9 (2017: Theorem 11.2 to Proposition 11.5)). §11.3 pushes forward and pulls back functors $\Theta : X \to \mathbb Q^+$ (Definition 11.3.1, Lemma 11.3.2, Theorem 11.3.3 (2017: Definition 11.4, Lemma 11.5, Theorem 11.3)) and continues with the bundles of tangent sets and orientations (Lemma 11.3.4 to Theorem 11.3.9 (2017: Lemma 11.6 to Theorem 11.7)). §11.4 transforms the canonical measures (Theorems 11.4.1, 11.4.2, 11.4.4, 11.4.5, Remark 11.4.3 (2017: Theorems 11.8, 11.9, Remark 11.3)). §11.5 carries auxiliary norms and sc⁺-multisections along generalized strong bundle isomorphisms (Proposition 11.5.1, Lemma 11.5.2, Proposition 11.5.3 (2017: Proposition 11.6, Lemma 11.7, Proposition 11.7)). §11.6 studies proper covering functors up to equivalence (Lemmas 11.6.1–11.6.3, Definitions 11.6.4, 11.6.5, 11.6.7, Theorem 11.6.6, Proposition 11.6.8, Remark 11.6.9 (2017: Lemmas 11.8, 11.9, Definitions 11.6–11.8, Theorem 11.10, Proposition 11.8, Remark 11.4); Lemma 11.6.1 is new in the 2021 edition).

**Six structural choices.** (1) *Faces by their object sets.* A face $X_\theta$ of $X$ is the saturated set $\pi^{-1}(\theta)$ with the morphisms whose source lies in it; the faces of $|X|$ are Chapter 2's faces for the index $d_{|X|}$ on the open set $|X|$, so Chapter 2's lemmas on faces apply to them (§XI.2). (2) *Total push-forwards.* $F_*\Theta$ is defined for every sc-smooth functor and every map on objects, with one choice of $x$ and of a morphism $F(x) \to y$, and $0$ where there is none; the statements say when the choice does not matter (§XI.5). (3) *Classes through representatives.* $f_*$ and $f^*$ on $\mathcal{EP}(E^{-1})$ are computed on a chosen representative with an equivalence as left leg (`rep`), and shown independent of it (§XI.6). (4) *Multisections as functors.* A multisection functor on a strong bundle is a map on the total object space constant along the morphisms of the total ep-groupoid; local section structures are not formalized (§XI.11). (5) *Functors of proper coverings as pairs.* A functor $F \to F'$ is a structure with the two functors and the two commutation equations, on objects and on morphisms, and $\mathcal{PC}$ is a category with composition associative on the nose (§§XI.13, XI.15). (6) *Sections and boundary operations.* Section functors of $\widehat{Gr}$ are functions on all objects, $0$ off $X_\infty$; Chapter 9's boundary orientation depends on a boundary operation given as data, so Theorem 11.3.9 carries a hypothesis relating the boundary operations on the two sides (§XI.8).

### XI.18 What the formalization taught us

Most of Chapter 11 is Chapter 10's machinery applied to one more kind of object, and the formal work went into the two places where an object is carried along an equivalence by a choice. For functors $\Theta$ the choice of $x$ and $F(x) \to y$ does not matter as soon as $F$ is full, which is all that the well-definedness in Lemma 11.3.2 uses; the statements for generalized maps then reduce, through one base change formula in the weak fibred product, to statements for equivalences, and the pull-back is defined for every generalized map. For branched ep⁺-subgroupoids the text's proof moves a local branching structure by $\hat\psi \circ F$; written out, the sheets must first be cut down to the domain of that local sc-diffeomorphism, and the paracompactness of the support, part of 2021 Definition 9.1.2, must be carried along too. Theorem 11.3.3 (6) refers to the uniqueness of dimensional decompositions, which does not hold as we read it (Chapter 9); the statement for every decomposition is what the proof gives. The rest of §11.3, whose proofs the text leaves to the reader, needed Chapter 9's notions between two M-polyfolds rather than on one, Chapter 9's independence hypothesis carried along, and, for Theorem 11.3.9, a condition on the boundary operations that Chapter 9 takes as data. Faces are a question about $|X|$ alone: once the faces of $|X|$ are Chapter 2's faces for $d_{|X|}$, Remark 11.1.5 and the bijections of Proposition 11.1.6 are statements about homeomorphisms preserving an index, and hold for every generalized isomorphism; only the ep-groupoid structure of a face needs more. In §11.6 the fibre condition of Definition 11.6.4 (2) comes for free for proper coverings, by unique lifting; and the chain of natural equivalences in Proposition 11.6.8 needs one more step than it displays.
