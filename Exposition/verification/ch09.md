**V9.1 (Definition 9.1.2 (2017: 9.1), branched ep⁺-subgroupoids; `Polyfold.EpGroupoid.IsFunctorial`, `Polyfold.EpGroupoid.supp`, `Polyfold.EpGroupoid.orbitSupp`, `Polyfold.EpGroupoid.LocalBranchingStructure`, `Polyfold.EpGroupoid.IsBranchedEpSubgroupoid`, `Polyfold.EpGroupoid.isBranchedEpSubgroupoid_zero`, `Polyfold.EpGroupoid.localBranchingStructure_indicator`, `Polyfold.EpGroupoid.isBranchedEpSubgroupoid_indicator`, `Polyfold.rnPolyfold_isSmoothSubmanifold_singleton`).** A branched ep⁺-subgroupoid is a functor $\Theta \ge 0$ whose support lies in the smooth set, whose orbit support $|\mathrm{supp}(\Theta)|$ is paracompact, and which at every $x \in \mathrm{supp}(\Theta)$ has a local branching structure: an open $U(x)$, finitely many smooth submanifolds $M_i \subseteq U(x)$ through $x$ with proper inclusions, weights $\sigma_i > 0$, and $\Theta(y) = \sum_{y \in M_i} \sigma_i$ on $U(x)$. The zero functor and the indicator of a smooth point of $\mathbb R^n$ are examples. *Book:* Definition 9.1.2, p. 402 (2017: Definition 9.1, p. 292). *Hidden hypotheses:* none. *Deviation (restated):* $\Theta$ takes values in $\mathbb Q$ with $\Theta \ge 0$ a field; "the topology induced on $|\mathrm{supp}(\Theta)|$ is paracompact" is a `ParacompactSpace` instance on the subtype; "the inclusion $M_i \to U(x)$ is proper" is `IsProperMap` of the inclusion. The 2017 text's "(ii) and (iii)" in (4) is "(2) and (3)" in 2021 (possible misprint, corrected) (Findings, item 1). *Update (2026-11-12):* by Remark 9.1.4 the paracompactness in (1) holds when $|\mathrm{supp}(\Theta)|$ is component compact (`Polyfold.EpGroupoid.isBranchedEpSubgroupoid_of_isComponentCompact`, V9.63; gap G.94 closed).
