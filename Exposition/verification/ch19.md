**V19.1 (Definition 19.1.1 (2017: 19.1), groupoidal categories; `Polyfold.GroupoidalCategory`, `Polyfold.GroupoidalCategory.definition_19_1_1`).** A groupoidal category is Chapter 17's `GroupoidalCategory` (Definition 17.1.1): a groupoid structure on `Obj`, `Mor` with finite morphism sets between any two objects; `definition_19_1_1` spells out that every morphism is invertible and that the morphism sets are finite. *Book:* §19.1, Definition 19.1.1, p. 863 (2017: Definition 19.1). *Hidden hypotheses:* none. *Deviation (same):* The text's remark that the orbit space is a set is a Lean type `C.OrbitSpace`.

**V19.2 (Definition 19.1.2 (2017: 19.2), uniformizers at an object; `Polyfold.GroupoidalCategory.Uniformizer`, `Polyfold.GroupoidalCategory.Uniformizer.IsAt`, `Polyfold.GroupoidalCategory.Uniformizer.IsCentredAt`, `Polyfold.GroupoidalCategory.UniformizerConstruction.IsCentred`).** `Uniformizer`: an M-polyfold `O` with a finite group `G` acting by sc-diffeomorphisms and a functor $G \ltimes O \to S$, injective on objects and fully faithful; `IsAt α` is (3), some $\bar o$ with $\Psi(\bar o) = \alpha$. *Book:* §19.1, Definition 19.1.2, p. 864 (2017: Definition 19.2). *Hidden hypotheses:* none. *Deviation (restated):* "α an object with isotropy group G" is the separate predicate `IsCentredAt` ($G$ fixes $\bar o$, so that $G \cong \mathrm{Aut}(\alpha)$ by full faithfulness) and `IsCentred` for a whole construction; nothing in §§19.1–19.5 needs it, so the definition is taken without it, which is more general. As the text says, no topology is required; the domain `O` is Hausdorff and paracompact as an M-polyfold.

**V19.3 (Definition 19.1.3 (2017: 19.3), uniformizer constructions; `Polyfold.GroupoidalCategory.UniformizerConstruction`, `Polyfold.GroupoidalCategory.UniformizerConstruction.Unif`, `Polyfold.GroupoidalCategory.UniformizerConstruction.HasUniformizers`, `Polyfold.GroupoidalCategory.UniformizerConstruction.HasClassUniformizers`, `Polyfold.GroupoidalCategory.UniformizerConstruction.HasUniformizers.hasClassUniformizers`).** A uniformizer construction is a family `F α` of uniformizers at α (field `isAt`); `Unif` is the type of pairs $(\Psi, \alpha)$ with $\Psi \in F(\alpha)$. `HasUniformizers` ($F(\alpha) \ne \emptyset$ for all α) implies `HasClassUniformizers` (every class meets an α with $F(\alpha) \ne \emptyset$). *Book:* §19.1, Definition 19.1.3, p. 865 (2017: Definition 19.3). *Hidden hypotheses:* none. *Deviation (same):* A functor $S^- \to \mathrm{SET}$ is a family indexed by objects. The non-emptiness is not part of the definition here; it is the hypothesis of V19.12.

**V19.4 (Remark 19.1.4 (2017: 19.1), $F$ as a functor on $S$; `Polyfold.GroupoidalCategory.UniformizerConstruction.Functorial`, `Polyfold.GroupoidalCategory.UniformizerConstruction.remark_19_1_4`).** `Functorial`: maps $F(\varphi) : F(\alpha) \to F(\alpha')$ with $F(1_\alpha) = \mathrm{id}$ and $F(\varphi' \circ \varphi) = F(\varphi') \circ F(\varphi)$; `remark_19_1_4`: each $F(\varphi)$ is a bijection. *Book:* §19.1, Remark 19.1.4, p. 865 (2017: Remark 19.1). *Hidden hypotheses:* none. *Deviation (same):* The geometric meaning of $F(\varphi)$ is prose. Nothing later uses the functoriality.

**V19.5 (The transition sets and the structure maps $s, t, u, \iota, m$, p. 865; `Polyfold.GroupoidStr.TransitionSet`, `Polyfold.GroupoidStr.TransitionSet.unit`, `Polyfold.GroupoidStr.TransitionSet.inv`, `Polyfold.GroupoidStr.TransitionSet.mul`, `Polyfold.GroupoidStr.TransitionSet.mul_eq_comp`, `Polyfold.GroupoidalCategory.Uniformizer.Trans`, `Polyfold.GroupoidalCategory.Uniformizer.hat`).** $M(\Psi, \Psi')$ is the weak fibred product, triples $(o, \varphi, o')$ with $\varphi : \Psi(o) \to \Psi'(o')$ (Chapter 17's `TransitionSet`, fields `src`, `mor`, `tgt`), with the unit, the inversion and the multiplication; $\bar\Psi(g, o) = (o, \Psi(g, o), g * o)$ is `hat`. *Book:* §19.1, p. 865 (2017: §19.1). *Hidden hypotheses:* none. *Deviation (same):* none.

**V19.6 (Properties (19.3) (A)–(F) of transition data, pp. 866–867; `Polyfold.GroupoidalCategory.UniformizerConstruction.TransitionData`, `Polyfold.GroupoidalCategory.UniformizerConstruction.TransitionData.fmap`, `Polyfold.GroupoidalCategory.UniformizerConstruction.TransitionData.fmap_src`, `Polyfold.GroupoidalCategory.UniformizerConstruction.TransitionData.continuousOn_fmap`, `Polyfold.GroupoidalCategory.UniformizerConstruction.TransitionData.isOpen_image_fmap`, `Polyfold.GroupoidalCategory.UniformizerConstruction.TransitionData.isScDiffeoOn_fmap_restrict`, `Polyfold.GroupoidalCategory.UniformizerConstruction.TransitionData.exists_nbhd_fmap`, `Polyfold.GroupoidalCategory.UniformizerConstruction.TransitionData.mul_fmap`).** `TransitionData`: for every $h \in M(\Psi, \Psi')$ an open `dom` $V(o) \ni s(h)$ and a germ `map` $F_h : V(o) \to M(\Psi, \Psi')$ with $s \circ F_h = \mathrm{id}$, $F_h(s(h)) = h$; (A) the special form on $M(\Psi, \Psi)$ (`map_hat`), (B) $f_h = t \circ F_h$ an sc-diffeomorphism onto an open set (`diffeo`), (C) `identity`, (D) `inversion`, (E) `multiplication`, (F) the Hausdorff property (`hausdorff`), and the stability under restriction to $V(o)$ (`stability`). `fmap` is $f_h$; the first identity of (E), $f_{h'} \circ f_h = f_{m(h', h)}$, follows from the second (`mul_fmap`). *Book:* §19.1, properties (19.3), pp. 866–867 (2017: §19.1). *Hidden hypotheses:* none. *Deviation (same):* (D) and (E) are stated on the domains where both sides are defined, as the text intends.

**V19.7 (Restriction of transition data, p. 867, and Definition 19.1.5 (new in the 2021 edition), equivalence; `Polyfold.GroupoidalCategory.UniformizerConstruction.TransitionData.restrict`, `Polyfold.GroupoidalCategory.UniformizerConstruction.TransitionData.Equiv`, `Polyfold.GroupoidalCategory.UniformizerConstruction.TransitionData.equiv_refl`, `Polyfold.GroupoidalCategory.UniformizerConstruction.TransitionData.Equiv.symm`, `Polyfold.GroupoidalCategory.UniformizerConstruction.TransitionData.Equiv.trans`, `Polyfold.GroupoidalCategory.UniformizerConstruction.TransitionData.setoid`, `Polyfold.GroupoidalCategory.UniformizerConstruction.TransitionData.equiv_restrict`).** Replacing every $V(o)$ by a smaller open $Q(o) \ni o$ gives transition data (`restrict`); $F \sim F'$ if for every $h$ there is an open $V \ni s(h)$ in both domains with $F_h = F'_h$ on $V$; this is an equivalence relation, and a restriction is equivalent to the original. *Book:* §19.1, p. 867, and Definition 19.1.5, p. 867 (new in the 2021 edition). *Hidden hypotheses:* none. *Deviation (same):* "Restrictions to suitable smaller neighbourhoods coincide" is read per $h$.

**V19.8 (Definition 19.1.6 (2017: 19.4), transition constructions; `Polyfold.GroupoidalCategory.UniformizerConstruction.TransitionConstruction`).** `TransitionConstruction := Quotient TransitionData.setoid`. *Book:* §19.1, Definition 19.1.6, p. 867 (2017: Definition 19.4). *Hidden hypotheses:* none. *Deviation (same):* In 2017 a transition construction was the data itself; in 2021 it is an equivalence class, as here.
