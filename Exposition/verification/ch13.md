### Sign-off items

1. (V13.1) For a neighbourhood with the natural action and proper closure, $\mathrm{cl}_X(V) \subset U(x)$ if and only if $\mathrm{cl}_{|X|}|V| \subset |U(x)|$.
2. (V13.2) A good system: natural neighbourhoods with proper closures whose morphism components are sc-diffeomorphisms under $s$ and $t$.
3. (V13.3) Invariant open shrinkings of a good system are good systems, and invariant refinements exist.
4. (V13.4) Refinements of good systems and good systems subordinate to a cover.
5. (V13.5) The natural bi-action of $G_{x'} \times G_x$ on $\mathcal U(x,x')$ is an action by sc-smooth maps.
6. (V13.6) The bi-action permutes the components of $\mathcal U(x,x')$ freely on each side, and their images under $s$ and $t$ are equal or disjoint.
7. (V13.7) A good system on $Q$ with $|Q| = |X|$, proper in $X$, extends to a good system of $X$ inside given neighbourhoods.
8. (V13.8) For paracompact $|X|$ there is a good system inside any given neighbourhoods.
9. (V13.9) A component $\Sigma$ determines a unique isomorphism of stabilisers $G_{x,\Sigma} \to G_{x',\Sigma}$ intertwining $D_\Sigma$.
10. (V13.10) Domain support and support of an sc⁺-multisection; the support is finite over each point with weights summing to 1.
11. (V13.11) The pointwise norm $N(\Lambda)(x)$ is attained, and the domain support is $\mathrm{cl}\{N(\Lambda) > 0\}$.
12. (V13.12) $\Lambda$ is trivial on $V$ when $\Lambda(0_x) = 1$ there; the domain support is the least closed set outside which $\Lambda$ is trivial.
13. (V13.13) A symmetric sc⁺-section structure at $x$: a natural representation, sections indexed by a finite $G_x$-set, equivariant, representing $\Lambda$.
14. (V13.14) Every sc⁺-multisection admits a symmetric sc⁺-section structure at every point.
15. (V13.15) The convolution $\Lambda' \oplus \Lambda''$ of sc⁺-multisections is an sc⁺-multisection; $\oplus$ is associative and commutative.
16. (V13.16) Scalar multiples $a\Lambda$ of sc⁺-multisections are sc⁺-multisections.
17. (V13.17) $\beta\Lambda$ is an sc⁺-multisection for every sc-smooth functor $\beta$.
18. (V13.18) Locally finite families of sc⁺-multisections, with stable index sets.
19. (V13.19) The sum of a locally finite family of sc⁺-multisections is an sc⁺-multisection.
20. (V13.20) Local sc-diffeomorphisms injective on open sets are sc-diffeomorphisms onto their images; natural neighbourhoods with proper closure exist.
