## Chapter 11 (HWZ, Part II, §§11.1, 11.3, 11.5, 11.6 (2017 §§11.1, 11.3, 11.5, 11.6)): verified for the tangent functor and faces up to equivalence, push-forwards and pull-backs of branched ep⁺-subgroupoids with their tangents, boundaries and orientations, auxiliary norms and multisection functors under generalized strong bundle isomorphisms, and proper coverings up to equivalence, with the readings recorded below, except the ep-groupoid structure of faces, the boundary operations of Theorem 11.3.9 along equivalences, conditions (1) and (2) of auxiliary norms, the local section structures of sc⁺-multisections and the localizations of §11.6; §11.2 (sc-differential forms) and §11.4 (integration) are not verified

Chapter 11 (pp. 521–566 (2017 pp. 375–416); numbers and pages are those of the book (Springer 2021), with the 2017 text's in parentheses) studies how the notions of Chapters 8 and 9 behave under equivalences and generalized maps. §11.1 extends the tangent functor to $\mathcal{EP}(E^{-1})$ (Theorem 11.1.1 (2017: 11.1)), shows that generalized isomorphisms preserve the degeneracy index (Proposition 11.1.2 (2017: 11.1)), defines faces in the ep-groupoid sense (Definition 11.1.3 (2017: 11.1), Example 11.1.4 (2017: 11.1)) and shows that equivalences and generalized isomorphisms induce bijections of faces (Remark 11.1.5, Proposition 11.1.6, Remark 11.1.7, Proposition 11.1.8 (2017: Remark 11.1, Proposition 11.2, Remark 11.2, Proposition 11.3)). §11.2 carries sc-differential forms along equivalences and generalized maps (Theorem 11.2.1, Lemmas 11.2.2, 11.2.4–11.2.6, Definitions 11.2.3, 11.2.7, Propositions 11.2.8, 11.2.9 (2017: Theorem 11.2, Lemmas 11.1–11.4, Definitions 11.2, 11.3, Propositions 11.4, 11.5)). §11.3 defines the pull-back and the push-forward of functors $\Theta : X \to \mathbb Q^+$ along equivalences (Definition 11.3.1, Lemma 11.3.2 (2017: Definition 11.4, Lemma 11.5)) and along generalized isomorphisms (pp. 536–537), shows that they preserve branched ep⁺-subgroupoids and their properties (Theorem 11.3.3 (2017: 11.3)), and continues with section functors of the bundles of oriented tangent sets, orientations, tangents and boundaries (Lemma 11.3.4 to Theorem 11.3.9 (2017: Lemma 11.6 to Theorem 11.7)). §11.4 transforms the canonical measures and boundary measures (Theorems 11.4.1, 11.4.4 (2017: 11.8, 11.9), Remark 11.4.3 (2017: 11.3), and the effective versions, Theorems 11.4.2 and 11.4.5, new in the 2021 edition). §11.5 carries auxiliary norms and sc⁺-multisections along generalized strong bundle isomorphisms (Proposition 11.5.1, Lemma 11.5.2, Proposition 11.5.3 (2017: Proposition 11.6, Lemma 11.7, Proposition 11.7)). §11.6 studies proper covering functors up to equivalence: their morphism maps (Lemma 11.6.1, new in the 2021 edition), the fibres as translation groupoids (Lemmas 11.6.2, 11.6.3 (2017: 11.8, 11.9)), functors, equivalences and natural transformations between proper coverings (Definitions 11.6.4, 11.6.5 (2017: 11.6, 11.7)), the weak fibred product (Theorem 11.6.6 (2017: 11.10)), diagrams and their common refinements (Definition 11.6.7, Proposition 11.6.8 (2017: Definition 11.8, Proposition 11.8)), the localization $\mathcal{PC}(E^{-1})$ (pp. 561–563) and, in Remark 11.6.9 (2017: 11.4), proper strong bundle coverings.

What is verified. In §11.1 Theorem 11.1.1, Proposition 11.1.2, Remark 11.1.7 and Definition 11.1.3 are verified as stated, the last with faces recorded by their object sets (V11.1–V11.3, V11.7); Example 11.1.4 holds for $[0, \infty)^2 \oplus W$ with any sc-Banach space $W$ (V11.4); the bijections of faces of Remark 11.1.5, quoted there from the literature, are proved (V11.5); Proposition 11.1.6 is verified for the bijection $[d]_*$ and the maps of orbit spaces it induces on faces, and Proposition 11.1.8 with the maps on faces read as restrictions of $|f|$ (V11.6, V11.8). In §11.3 Definition 11.3.1, Lemma 11.3.2 in a stronger form, the push-forward and pull-back along generalized maps with their functorial properties, and Theorem 11.3.3, with (6) read as below, are verified (V11.14–V11.19); so are Lemma 11.3.4 with (11.8) and (11.9), Theorem 11.3.5, Definition 11.3.6 with (11.10), and Theorems 11.3.7 and 11.3.8, under Chapter 9's hypothesis that the tangent sums of Theorem 9.2.1 are independent, and Theorem 11.3.9 for a representative under a boundary-transfer hypothesis (V11.20–V11.26). In §11.5 Proposition 11.5.1 is verified without conditions (1) and (2) of Definition 8.3.5, and Lemma 11.5.2 and Proposition 11.5.3 for the functors underlying sc⁺-multisections (V11.30–V11.32). In §11.6 Lemmas 11.6.1–11.6.3, Definitions 11.6.4, 11.6.5, 11.6.7, Theorem 11.6.6 in all its claims and Proposition 11.6.8 are verified (V11.33–V11.41). Several places of the text needed more than a reading. (a) *Theorem 11.1.1 (2017: 11.1).* The text checks that $T$ respects refinements; that $T$ preserves composition needs a comparison functor $T(A \times_Y B) \to TA \times_{TY} TB$, an equivalence by two out of three (V11.1). (b) *Proposition 11.1.6 (2017: 11.2).* Only the part on $[d]_*$ is formalized; the faces are not built as ep-groupoids (V11.6). (c) *Theorem 11.3.3 (2017: 11.3).* The paracompactness of $|\mathrm{supp}(\Theta)|$, which 2021 Definition 9.1.2 (1) asks for, is proved for push-forwards and pull-backs, though the text's proof does not mention it, and the sheets of a local branching structure are first restricted to the domain of the local sc-diffeomorphism (V11.17); the uniqueness part of Proposition 9.1.11 does not hold as we read it (Chapter 9), so (6) is read as "every dimensional decomposition is mapped to one" (V11.18). (d) *Lemma 11.3.2 (2017: 11.5).* The well-definedness of $F_*$ needs only fullness, (2) only essential surjectivity (V11.15). (e) *Definition 11.6.4 (2017: 11.6).* For proper covering functors the fibre condition of (2) follows from the other two conditions (V11.36). (f) *Theorem 11.6.6 (2017: 11.10).* Step (C) is proved through the properness of $H$ on objects instead of sequences (V11.38). (g) *Theorems 11.3.7 and 11.3.8 (2017: 11.5, 11.6).* Their proofs, left to the reader, need Chapter 9's tangent, orientation and boundary notions carried by sc-diffeomorphisms between different M-polyfolds; the independence of the tangent sums, assumed for $\Theta$, is carried to $f_*\Theta$ and $f^*\Theta'$ (V11.23, V11.24). (h) *Theorem 11.3.9 (2017: 11.7).* Chapter 9's boundary orientation depends on a boundary operation taken as data, so the theorem is stated for a representative and boundary operations corresponding under the transfer maps (V11.25). (i) *Proposition 11.6.8 (2017: 11.8).* The displayed chain for the functors $\varepsilon$ stops one step short; the last step uses the fourth assumption (V11.41). Possible misprints are recorded in V11.2, V11.14, V11.17, V11.34 and V11.38. Not verified: the ep-groupoid structure of the faces, the intersection clause and the generalized isomorphisms $[d]_F$ of Proposition 11.1.6; §11.2; that the boundary operations of Definition 9.3.8 satisfy the boundary-transfer hypothesis along equivalences, and so a form of Theorem 11.3.9 independent of the representative; §11.4; conditions (1) and (2) of Definition 8.3.5 in Proposition 11.5.1; the local section structures of sc⁺-multisections in Lemma 11.5.2 and Proposition 11.5.3; the localization $\mathcal{PC}(E^{-1})$ and the category of Remark 11.6.9 (gaps G.128–G.135).

Chapter 9's `Polyfold.EpGroupoid.HasIndependentTangentSums`, the independence part of Theorem 9.2.1, which Chapter 9's formalization assumes and proves on $\mathbb R^n$ (`Polyfold.EpGroupoid.hasIndependentTangentSums_rn`), is assumed for the branched ep⁺-subgroupoid being moved in Theorems 11.3.5, 11.3.7–11.3.9 and Definition 11.3.6, and proved for its push-forward and pull-back. `Polyfold.MPolyfold.IsBoundaryTransfer`, a pointwise condition on the given transfer maps and boundary operations, is assumed in Theorem 11.3.9; it holds along the identity (`Polyfold.MPolyfold.isBoundaryTransfer_id`). The tangent functor on $\mathcal{EP}(E^{-1})$ is defined on the ep-groupoids of the universe that Chapter 8's tangent functor uses ($\max(u, 1)$). Several statements need less than the text assumes: Lemma 11.3.2 (V11.15), Proposition 11.1.6, which holds for every generalized isomorphism (V11.6), and Theorem 11.6.6 (1) (V11.38).
