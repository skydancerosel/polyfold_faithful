Chapter 15 (pp. 683–703; §§15.1–15.3 are pp. 683–700; numbers and pages are those of the book (Springer 2021), with the 2017 text's numbers in parentheses) is transversality and the invariants for sc-Fredholm section functors perturbed by sc⁺-multisections. §15.1 defines the linearization $T(f, \Lambda)$ with values in $\mathrm{GrF}$ (preamble, Theorem 15.1.1, Remark 15.1.2, Definition 15.1.7 (2017: Theorem 15.1, Remark 15.1, Definition 15.1)), proved through separating sequences (Proposition 15.1.3, Lemmas 15.1.4, 15.1.5, Remark 15.1.6 (2017: Proposition 15.1, Lemmas 15.1, 15.2; the remark is new in 2021)). §15.2 (2017: §15.2) defines transversality and good position and describes the local solution sets (Definition 15.2.1, Theorem 15.2.2, Remark 15.2.3, Definition 15.2.4, Theorem 15.2.5, Remark 15.2.6 (2017: Definition 15.2, Theorem 15.2, Remark 15.2, Definition 15.3, Theorem 15.3, Remark 15.3)); §15.3 constructs perturbations (Definitions 15.3.1, 15.3.3, 15.3.5, Theorems 15.3.7, 15.3.9, Corollary 15.3.10 (2017: Definitions 15.4–15.6, Theorems 15.4, 15.5, Corollary 15.1)); §15.4 treats orientations and invariants. §15.2 and part of §15.3 were added in Part III wave 2.
