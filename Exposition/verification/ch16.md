### Sign-off items

1. (V16.1) A polyfold structure on $Z$ is an ep-groupoid with paracompact orbit space satisfying (ZHOU) and a homeomorphism $|X| \to Z$; $Z$ is then metrizable (V16.14).
2. (V16.2) Equivalence of polyfold structures through a generalized isomorphism compatible with the homeomorphisms is an equivalence relation.
3. (V16.3) The generalized isomorphism of an equivalence need not be unique (an example on $\mathbb R$ with $\mathbb Z/3$ isotropy); it is unique for an effective target.
4. (V16.4) A polyfold is a space with an equivalence class of polyfold structures.
5. (V16.5) Equivalent polyfold structures both admit, or both do not admit, sc-smooth bump functions and sc-smooth partitions of unity.
6. (V16.6) A polyfold admits bump functions (partitions of unity) if one, equivalently every, structure in its class does.
7. (V16.7) Over a homeomorphism there can be two generalized isomorphisms (the example of V16.3); there is at most one for an effective target.
8. (V16.8) Equivalent structures give the same degeneracy index on $Z$.
9. (V16.9) The degeneracy index $d_Z$ of a polyfold.
10. (V16.10) The boundary $\partial Z = \{d_Z \ge 1\}$.
11. (V16.11) Tameness of polyfold structures is invariant under equivalence.
12. (V16.12) A polyfold is tame if one, equivalently every, structure in its class is tame.
13. (V16.13) $\mathbb R$ with the trivial $\mathbb Z/3$-action is a polyfold structure with paracompact orbit space.
14. (V16.14) The underlying space of a polyfold structure is metrizable.
15. (V16.15) Equal underlying maps give equal orbit maps $|\mathfrak f| = |\mathfrak g|$, $|T\mathfrak f| = |T\mathfrak g|$, and equal $\mathfrak f$, $T\mathfrak f$ for an effective target; not in general.
16. (V16.16) The tangent space $T(Z, c)$ and the bijections $T\alpha$, independent of the generalized isomorphism, with (16.5).
17. (V16.17) $T(Z, c)$ has a unique topology making every $T\alpha$ a homeomorphism, and it is metrizable.
18. (V16.18) The tangent polyfold $(T(Z, c), Tc)$.
19. (V16.19) The push-forward of forms does not depend on the generalized isomorphism with the given orbit map.
20. (V16.20) Sc-differential forms on a polyfold, as classes of forms on its structures, with their vector-space structure.
21. (V16.21) Sc-differential forms on a polyfold form a de Rham complex.
22. (V16.22) Branched suborbifolds of a polyfold, and the well-defined function $\theta$ on $Z$.
23. (V16.23) Closed, compact, manifold-type, orbifold-type and $n$-dimensional branched suborbifolds; tame as "some representative".
24. (V16.24) $\mathrm{supp}\,\theta$ is homeomorphic to $|\mathrm{supp}\,\Theta|$ and carries the structure of Proposition 9.1.9, for every representative.
25. (V16.25) Oriented branched suborbifolds of a polyfold.
26. (V16.26) The boundary of an oriented branched suborbifold is well defined.
27. (V16.27) The integrals over tame compact oriented branched suborbifolds do not depend on the tame compact representative.
