Chapter 16 (pp. 705–740; §16.1 is pp. 705–715; numbers and pages are those of the book (Springer 2021), with the 2017 text's numbers in parentheses) defines polyfolds and their structures. §16.1 defines polyfold structures (Definition 16.1.1 (2017: 16.1)), their equivalence (Definition 16.1.2 (2017: 16.2)) and polyfolds (Definition 16.1.3 (2017: 16.3)), shows that bump functions and partitions of unity do not depend on the structure (Proposition 16.1.4, Definition 16.1.5 (2017: Proposition 16.2, Definition 16.4)), discusses generalized isomorphisms over homeomorphisms (Remark 16.1.7 (2017: 16.1)), and defines the degeneracy index, the boundary and tameness (Lemma 16.1.13, Definitions 16.1.14, 16.1.17, Lemma 16.1.19, Definition 16.1.20 (2017: Lemma 16.1, Definitions 16.9, 16.11, Lemma 16.2, Definition 16.12)).

What is verified. Definitions 16.1.1–16.1.3, 16.1.5, 16.1.14, 16.1.17, 16.1.20 and Lemma 16.1.13 are verified as stated (V16.1, V16.2, V16.4, V16.6, V16.8–V16.10, V16.12); Proposition 16.1.4 with bump functions on the object M-polyfold (V16.5); Lemma 16.1.19 as an iff (V16.11). ### Sign-off items

1. (V16.1) A polyfold structure on $Z$ is an ep-groupoid with paracompact orbit space satisfying (ZHOU) and a homeomorphism $|X| \to Z$.
2. (V16.2) Equivalence of polyfold structures through a generalized isomorphism compatible with the homeomorphisms is an equivalence relation.
3. (V16.3) The generalized isomorphism of an equivalence need not be unique (an example on $\mathbb R$ with $\mathbb Z/3$ isotropy); it is unique for an effective target.
4. (V16.4) A polyfold is a space with an equivalence class of polyfold structures.
5. (V16.5) Equivalent polyfold structures both admit, or both do not admit, sc-smooth bump functions and sc-smooth partitions of unity.
6. (V16.6) A polyfold admits bump functions (partitions of unity) if one, equivalently every, structure in its class does.
7. (V16.7) Over a homeomorphism there can be two generalized isomorphisms (the example of V16.3); there is at most one for an effective target.
8. (V16.8) Equivalent structures give the same degeneracy index on $Z$.
9. (V16.9) The degeneracy index $d_Z$ of a polyfold.
10. (V16.10) The boundary $\partial Z = \{d_Z \ge 1\}$.
11. (V16.11) Tameness of polyfold structures is invariant under equivalence.
12. (V16.12) A polyfold is tame if one, equivalently every, structure in its class is tame.
13. (V16.13) $\mathbb R$ with the trivial $\mathbb Z/3$-action is a polyfold structure with paracompact orbit space.
